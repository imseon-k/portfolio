# 청춘해 Food Truck Apron Image

## Mode

Built-in image generation followed by deterministic compositing of the official `logo-standard.svg` and 8K finalization.

## Prompt

Create a premium photorealistic Samcheok Youth Mall lifestyle image of one young Korean food-truck worker grilling hot dogs. Crop tightly from just below the chin to the waist so no face or eyes are visible. The worker wears a clean warm off-white heavy-canvas bib apron with a broad, front-facing chest panel and a casual pale-aqua T-shirt. One hand uses stainless-steel tongs to turn hot dogs on the grill while the other holds a finished hot dog in a small tray. Shoot horizontally at 16:9 from a slightly low three-quarter angle, with the apron dominant at center-left, a shallow depth of field, and dynamic diagonal hand movement. Use bright soft daylight and a fresh, young, casual, playful mood, with accents inspired by East Sea Emerald `#00A0A2` and Samcheok Sunset Orange `#EF532D`. Include realistic woven fabric, toasted food, brushed steel, and subtle steam. Exclude visible faces, extra people, text, generated logos, watermarks, dark lighting, excessive smoke, clutter, distorted hands, duplicate tools, and floating food.

## Logo treatment

The apron was generated blank. The exact official SVG was then applied without redrawing or AI text generation, preserving its geometry and character forms. In the revised `v3` composite, the logo follows a four-corner perspective plane aligned to the apron, tilts with the upper bib edge, widens subtly toward the lower edge, and bends along the right-side vertical fabric fold. Brand colors are shaded by the underlying apron luminance, while locally sampled weave contrast modulates the print opacity so the cotton texture remains visible through the ink.
