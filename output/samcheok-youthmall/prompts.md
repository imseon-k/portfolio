# Samcheok Youth Mall mockup prompts

Mode: built-in image generation with supplied location, mood, and logo references. Final masters were cropped and upscaled to 3840 × 2160.

## Interior

Use case: photorealistic-natural  
Asset type: Samcheok Youth Mall branding portfolio interior hero image  
Primary request: Create a photorealistic architectural interior photograph showing a realistic contemporary redesign of the existing Samcheok Youth Mall second-floor food court.  
Input images: The actual food-court image is the structural anchor. Preserve its low ceiling, long central aisle, square cream columns, dark wood slat ceiling with linear lights, pale stone floor, glass-and-metal railings, and pale-oak material language. The three mood images guide the organic modular seating, biophilic planting, playful suspended lighting, soft curves, and youth-community atmosphere. The supplied official logo is reproduced as a dimensional wall sign.  
Scene: A recognizable, feasible renovation inside the existing traditional-market building.  
Subject: Curved modular seating in teal, ocean blue, pale mint, and restrained coral; lush planters; pale-oak tables; a few young adults using the space naturally.  
Composition: 16:9 wide architectural photograph, eye-level 24 mm lens, strong central depth, branded wall visible in the midground.  
Lighting: Bright neutral daylight mixed with warm linear ceiling lighting.  
Constraints: Preserve the venue's realistic dimensions and low ceiling; no luxury-mall scale, fake text, extra logos, warped furniture, distorted people, heavy HDR, or watermark.

## Exterior

Use case: photorealistic-natural  
Asset type: Samcheok Youth Mall branding portfolio exterior hero image  
Primary request: Create a photorealistic architectural exterior-context photograph showing a realistic redesign of the Samcheok Youth Mall entrance from inside the existing traditional-market arcade.  
Input images: The actual market corridor is the structural anchor. Preserve the narrow aisle, translucent arched canopy, white steel ribs, pale-green two-story market wall, vendor stalls, hanging signs, lamps, shoppers, and authentic density. Constraints: Retain the real market's scale and visual density; no open plaza, generic street, luxury mall, empty corridor, oversized facade, excessive neon, distorted people, extra logos, or watermark.
